SQL behind this view 

      
Macros frame(init, lead), run(init), framebox(init, lead, y0, y1, x0, x1) and runbox(init, y0, y1, x0, x1) wrap read_zarr with ranges=; init is an ISO hour, lead is seconds, x, y are metres on the HRRR Lambert grid. Result rows with x, y columns are drawn on the map.
