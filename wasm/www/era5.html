ARCO ERA5 (1°, hourly, 1959-2022) in the browser

duckdb-wasm + locally built zarr extension. Store: /arco/ar/1959-2022-1h-360x181_equiangular_with_poles_conservative.zarr (proxied to GCS). Chunks are 8 hourly timesteps of the full 360x181 grid. Bound scans with ranges=['time:lo:hi', ...]. Bounds are raw coordinate values, or ISO dates on a dimension with CF units (time here is hours since 1959-01-01, so 2020-07-01 = 539088). Bounds are inclusive; chunks outside are never fetched and rows outside inside kept chunks are clipped, so no WHERE repeat is needed.
